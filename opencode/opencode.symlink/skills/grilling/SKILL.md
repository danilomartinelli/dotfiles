# Grilling

Before accepting a plan or diagnosis, ask:

- What exactly is the claim, and what evidence supports it?
- Which assumption is doing the most work?
- What observation would disprove it?
- What is the smallest experiment that separates the competing explanations?
- What risk, permission, or rollback boundary has been omitted?

Be direct but constructive. Do not manufacture objections; stop when the claim
is evidenced, bounded, and actionable. Record unresolved questions rather than
silently filling gaps.
